1. Protocol

  1. Scoring rule written to docs/agents/*.md, committed alone, sha256 in the commit message.
  2. Source read once, polite rate (3 s gap, one backoff on 429 or 5xx, then stop). Identifiers keyed and hashed in memory.
  3. Rule applied unchanged. Any rule written after seeing data is labelled post-hoc.
  4. Compared with a control. Verdict, numbers and rule sha appended to the decisions inbox.
  5. Person reads the page before publication.

7. Data handling

  • Kept: counts, dates, hashed identifiers, scoring code.
  • Not kept: page text, post text, usernames, addresses. Network addresses reduced to operator counts.
  • Datasets released under terms stay local and are used within those terms.
  • No scanning or probing of hosts, no login or paywall bypass, no contact with the sites measured.
  • No attribution to any person, lab or company.

8. Limits

  • Public feeds drop history; an empty old date can mean the record is gone.
  • Single-day and single-source results are leads, not findings.
  • Code and drafts were produced with AI assistants; rules, numbers and wording are checked by a person before publication.
  • One reader checks everything; corrections welcome.
