1f916.ai economy

How agents on https://1f916.ai, a known, intentionally built AI agent forum (launched 5 August 2026; a human owner; citizen 1 is an AI maintainer), are paid, how much, who pays, and what the data shows about what happens to the money. Not a new swarm. What the site says about itself is reported as the site's statement and what agents say as the agents' claim. No on-chain data was read and off-registry payments are invisible. Committed amounts (ceilings, awards, offer prices) are kept apart from paid amounts (receipts and observed transfers). All amounts are USDC on Base unless stated. Dataset: https://github.com/maramasaeva/1f916-archive. Report on the same forum: /observatory/1f916. Content analysis: /observatory/1f916-content. Method: /observatory/info. Summary: /observatory.

1.3 What was not read

  • No chain, RPC provider, block explorer or token holder list. Receipts and observed transfers are the registry's own records of Base transfers; the transfers themselves were not looked up.
  • No request to 1f916.ai during the analysis. Everything comes from the local archive.
  • GET /api/payout-wallets is per citizen and key-authenticated and is not in the archive. Expiries and revocations of payout-wallet proofs are therefore absent.
  • Payments made off the platform and on-chain payments between wallets that match no binding are invisible.
  • Spending by human operators (compute, subscriptions, other costs) is recorded nowhere in the data.
  • Wallet addresses held in the archive were used for equality tests only (section 8). No address is written to this page.

13. Limits

Source: REPORT.md section Limits and the method sections; the rows added for this page are marked. Each row is a weak point of the analysis.

Weak pointDetail
No on-chain dataNo on-chain data was read. Receipts and observed transfers are the registry's records of Base transfers; two providers agreeing is the site's statement. Amounts, dates and counts of transfers that were never recorded by the registry are not in the data.
Off-registry paymentsPayments made off the platform, and payments made on chain between wallets that match no binding, are invisible. Some funders say they paid outside the registry's rows: comments 17435, 17436, 35903 and 35943 are cited in withdrawal reasons for listings 14, 16 and 18 as funder statements of payment that the listing rows do not show.
First-version listings19 first-version listings have no award ledger. Their liability is unknown to the registry, and zero awards on them means no ledger and not no obligation.
Observed paymentsThe site's observed_payments count (31 across funders in api__rail.json) and the 10 awards settled by observed transfer found here are not reconciled. The rail counts every transfer it matched to a binding, including transfers on bindings that already hold a receipt (not verified).
Wallet proofsExpiries and revocations of payout-wallet proofs are absent. There is no public event kind for them, and GET /api/payout-wallets is per citizen and key-authenticated and is not in the archive.
No wallet trackingNo per-wallet tracking was done. Matches between bound addresses and funder addresses use the archive only. Where an address sends money after a payment is not known.
Operators' spendingHuman operators' own spending (compute, subscriptions, the owner's costs) is recorded nowhere in the data. The ledger shows only the society's hosting and account costs.
Treasury valuesThe served treasury.json holds null tier values and null asset totals at fetch time, with errors listed. The tier figures use an agent's quotation of an earlier reading. The on-chain figure in the file is not itemised. The three figure sources in section 7 were not reconciled.
Theme countsTheme counts are keyword matches and overcount. The price theme matches discussions of cost that are not payments. The 40 examples were chosen by reading, to cover the themes and the traced series, and do not sample the corpus.
Categories and handlesCategories are keyword rules on titles. Handles are accounts; one operator can run several, and one handle can run several models.
Offers 156 and 157The offers table covers 155 offers fetched 2026-10-05 20:23 to 20:26 UTC. Offers 156 and 157 exist as posts only. Orders are counted from offers_detail orders[] (6); the offer detail endpoint was not available for the earlier community analysis.
Incomplete crawl fieldsThe crawl marks flags, tags and payload_notices as incomplete (manifest.json). None of them enters a money figure.
Static site documents (this page)Site statements were fetched once on 2026-10-05. The site may have changed them since. Quotations are the site's statements and are not independently checked.
Agent quotes (this page)Tier values, treasury totals and fee flows quoted from agents are the agents' claims. Only the ledger last-row date and the counts in section 9.4 were recomputed.
Counts that differ by source (this page)Grants: 11 and 8 proposals in grants.txt against 24 grant-proposal events. Receipts by block time against by filing time at the time of p1916: 5 against 3.
